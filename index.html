Artist Biography

Alberto Luca Ghin

Based in Zurich, Alberto Luca Ghin specializes in Modern Pointillism on paper. His 2026 catalog focuses on uniform 30 × 42 cm oil studies investigating nocturnal light on lakes, high-altitude city perspectives, and contemporary financial skylines.

Poetics & Vision

Black as a Primordial Canvas & Active Substance

In my work, black is never a passive void or mere darkness, but a living, primordial substance. Acting like silence in music, it strips away daytime distractions, creating a dense, contemplative space where forms are preserved before being brought to light.

Pointillism as a Search for Essence

Through meticulous oil stippling on paper, each individual dot becomes an atomic quantum of energy. By rejecting hard outlines, forms emerge solely through the varying density and proximity of dots—a geometric process that captures the core essence of light and matter.

Night as the Revealer of Light

Exploring nocturnal visions allows light to act as a sculptor. Rather than illuminating everything indiscriminately, night reflections, urban glows, and luminous horizons emerge from the darkness with heightened intimacy, transforming each piece into a meditative mirror of the inner self.

The Synthesis of Rigor and Mysticism

My practice bridges two contrasting worlds: the painstaking precision of the pointillist technique and the ethereal, floating quality of the final image. The result is a process of emergence—building luminous, poetic forms directly out of the deep dark.

Using precise oil stippling on heavyweight fine art paper, each work builds complex optical colors that reveal subtle depth and texture.

Collector & Press Notes

Frequently Asked Questions

What medium and format are the 2026 works?

Every work in the 2026 catalog is an original Modern Pointillism oil study on fine art paper in a uniform 30 × 42 cm format, created in Zurich by Alberto Luca Ghin.

How can collectors, galleries, or curators arrange a viewing or acquisition?

Inquiries from Switzerland, the UK, the USA, Germany, France, and international art markets can be submitted directly through the Private Viewing Inquiry form on this site, routed to the studio.

What subjects does the studio focus on?

The 2026 catalog explores nocturnal light and reflections on Lake Zurich (Horgen) and Pula harbour (Lake View), aerial urban compositions of Palermo (Cities from the Sky), and financial district architecture such as Canary Wharf (Modern Skylines).
